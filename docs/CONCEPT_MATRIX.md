## 1. The 13-Point Weighted Rubric

Each candidate concept is scored from 1 (poor) to 10 (exceptional) across 13 weighted criteria (Total: 100 points maximum):

1. **Core Gameplay Quality & Clarity (Weight: 12%):** Is the minute-to-minute interaction immediately satisfying and clear?
2. **Immediate 10-Second Hook (Weight: 10%):** Does the player understand the goal and feel curiosity within 10 seconds?
3. **Replayability & Retention Loop (Weight: 10%):** Does failure trigger an immediate urge to retry ("one more run")?
4. **Originality & Innovation (Weight: 10%):** Does the game offer a fresh mechanic or unexpected twist?
5. **Theme Interpretation Adaptability (Weight: 10%):** How naturally can the core mechanic embody thematic concepts?
6. **Visual Identity & Contrast (Weight: 8%):** Will it stand out in itch.io thumbnail grids and screenshots?
7. **Audio Potential & Reactivity (Weight: 8%):** Can the sound design dynamically reinforce player actions and flow state?
8. **Juiciness & Game Feel (Weight: 8%):** Screen shake, hit pauses, particle feedback, kinetic responsiveness.
9. **Accessibility & Controls (Weight: 6%):** Unified keyboard, gamepad, and touch controls; clear affordances.
10. **Technical Feasibility (Weight: 6%):** 60 FPS guaranteed; zero reliance on unstable external dependencies.
11. **Scope Reliability (Weight: 4%):** Can a polished, complete build be achieved with 24 hours of buffer?
12. **Audience & Judge Appeal (Weight: 4%):** Aligned with itch.io jam evaluator habits and psychological rewards.
13. **Shareability & Streamability (Weight: 4%):** Does it generate memorable "clip-worthy" clutch moments?

## 2. Five Championship Concept Archetypes

### Archetype 1: *KINETIC SHADOW / REVERSE GRAVITY RUNNER*
- **One-Sentence Hook:** Manipulate your echo’s gravitational polarity to navigate lethal obstacle corridors in real-time sync.
- **Core Loop:** Player moves forward continuously; tapping space flips gravity, leaving an inverted ghost that triggers puzzle switches on opposite ceilings.
- **Why Judges Remember It:** Spatial mind-bending puzzle action combined with silky smooth momentum.
- **Rubric Score:** **91.2 / 100**

### Archetype 2: *PULSE RESONANCE / ARCADE WAVE DRIFTER*
- **One-Sentence Hook:** Propel an agile kinetic craft through neon hazard fields using directional sonic shockwaves instead of conventional thrusters.
- **Core Loop:** You don't have standard engines; clicking or pressing directional triggers emits blast rings that push you backwards while vaporizing nearby obstacles and detonating score multipliers.
- **First-Minute Experience:** Spawn in dark arena. Tap space -> dramatic kinetic blast waves emanate, lighting up walls, repelling enemies.
- **Replay Loop:** High-score combo chain counter; grazing hazards builds hyper-speed burst; instant retry upon impact.
- **Rubric Score:** **94.8 / 100** *(Highest Overall Game Feel & Polish Reliability)*

### Archetype 3: *CHRONO-WEAVER / MICRO-TIMELOOP DEDUCTION*
- **One-Sentence Hook:** Rewind a 10-second catastrophe room repeatedly to alter subtle physical domino chains and avert disaster.
- **Core Loop:** 10-second countdown runs; you move objects, press buttons, or place blockers; rewind to watch your past timeline versions execute actions simultaneously.
- **Why Judges Remember It:** Satisfying "aha!" puzzle moments, high innovation score.
- **Rubric Score:** **88.6 / 100** *(Higher production risk in level puzzle balancing)*

### Archetype 4: *MODULAR GLITCH COURIER*
- **One-Sentence Hook:** Deliver critical packets through an unstable digital mainframe where your own control inputs corrupt and swap upon taking damage.
- **Core Loop:** Top-down high-speed courier navigation. Getting hit doesn't kill you immediately; it swaps two control bindings (e.g. Turn Left becomes Dash), forcing frantic real-time adaptation.
- **Why Judges Remember It:** Humorous, chaotic, high innovation.
- **Rubric Score:** **86.4 / 100** *(Risk of player frustration if input scrambling is too harsh)*

### Archetype 5: *BIOLUMINESCENT VECTOR DEFENDER*
- **One-Sentence Hook:** Defend a fragile organic core by orbiting it with magnetic light tethers, whipping enemies into harmonic resonance traps.
- **Core Loop:** Physics-based circular tethering. Hook onto orbital nodes to whip and fling cosmic debris into encroaching voids.
- **Why Judges Remember It:** Hypnotic particle visuals and satisfying centrifugal physics.
- **Rubric Score:** **89.4 / 100**

## 4. Primary Recommendation & November 1 Activation Strategy

**Recommended Primary Base Architecture:** **Archetype 2: Pulse Resonance / Kinetic Vector Engine**

- **Theme Mapping Flexibility:**

  - If Theme = "CYCLE" / "LOOP": Energy pulse regenerates in cyclical orbital beats.
  - If Theme = "FUSION" / "SYNTHESIS": Dual-color pulses combine to solve opposing hazards.
  - If Theme = "LIMIT" / "EXPAND": Pulse range dictates safe perimeter boundaries.
